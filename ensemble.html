01 — Why Now

Two changes already happened. Most operating models haven't caught up with either.

The Clock

The EU AI Act's high-risk obligations landed 2 August 2026. DORA has been fully live since January 2025. NIS2 enforcement keeps spreading. This isn't a roadmap item anymore — it's three and a half weeks behind us.

The Tell

In June 2026, Scaled Agile rebuilt SAFe itself around AI as a core contributor. Its own practitioners still openly discuss "Why SAFe Hurts" — ceremony weight and role proliferation that predate AI entirely.

The Metric That Broke

When AI writes an estimated 30–70% of committed code, "deployment frequency" stops measuring what it used to. The old scoreboard needs new numbers — see Section 09.
